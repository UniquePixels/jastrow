Sha'ah Duration by Month

~40°N latitude. At equinox one sha'ah = exactly 60 min. Dashed line = equinox baseline.

Key point: Summer sha'ah ≈ 75 min; winter ≈ 46 min. All 12 sha'ot always span sunrise → sunset — only their clock-length changes.
