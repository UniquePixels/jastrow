# Scan comparison — 2026-11-01

The Claremont School of Theology 2021 pair (`dictionaryoftarg0001jast`,
`dictionaryoftarg0002unse`) against the pinned Robarts/UofT 2009 pair
(`dictionaryoftarg01jastuoft`, `dictionaryoftarg02jastuoft`), by eye,
on ten printed pages. Checklist group 6, box 3
([`../review-checklist-2026-10-25.md`](../review-checklist-2026-10-25.md)).
Facts only; the decision is D5.

## Method

- Pages chosen from the patch records: the six headwords annotated
  `illegible` in `checklist-batch-1-2026-10-22` (pp. 28, 116, 645,
  1072, 1265, 1404) and the four leaves most often linked from
  reviewed rationales (pp. 293, 394, 410, 1599). Six in vol. 1, four
  in vol. 2; every one is a dense two-column dictionary page.
- For each page the same crop was fetched from both scans at native
  resolution (IIIF region `pct:7,8,40,18`: the top of column a) and
  the whole leaf at 900 px wide to read the printed page number.
- The reads are this session's model reading the images, not a
  maintainer read. They are a comparison of sharpness, not evidence
  for any Hebrew reading (ruling `10-26 patch evidence`).

## Conclusion

On the eight leaves where the pinned scan is in focus the two pairs
are about the same: `jastuoft` carries 15–20 % more pixels per leaf
and is a little larger on pointing, Claremont has a little more
contrast, and vol. 2 is equal on all four pages. On two of the six
vol. 1 leaves (pp. 293 and 645) `jastuoft` is out of focus and the
pointing cannot be resolved, while Claremont is sharp on every leaf
read, so Claremont is clearly better on those leaves and not worse on
any. A switch costs the `columns.jsonl` leaf remap (vol. 1 shifts by
two leaves, vol. 2 keeps the same offset under a new identifier), the
IIIF regex in `sefaria-report.ts`, an amendment tranche for the IIIF
links and leaf cites in the reviewed records (the checklist counts 453
and 505; counted 2026-11-01 under `records/reviewed/`: 454 IIIF URLs
in rationale text and 566 `leaf` fields in evidence objects), and a
re-run of the L71 quote check.
